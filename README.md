# AI Lead Nurturing Agent

## Overview
The AI Lead Nurturing Agent helps businesses stay connected with potential customers through organized and personalized follow-ups.

It uses lead information such as name, email, service interest, budget, preferred contact time, and project timeline to support relevant communication.

## Features
- Organizes customer and lead information.
- Supports personalized follow-up messages.
- Helps maintain consistent communication.
- Uses customer interests to guide follow-ups.
- Supports the lead nurturing process.

## How It Works
1. Receive lead information.
2. Organize the customer's details.
3. Analyze their interests and requirements.
4. Prepare relevant follow-up communication.
5. Support continued engagement with potential customers.

## Use Cases
- Sales follow-ups
- Marketing agencies

## Project Goal
To automate parts of the lead nurturing process, improve follow-up consistency, and help businesses build stronger relationships with potential customers.
